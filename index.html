How it works

From your text to a finished site in four steps.

You keep the client and the relationship. Everything runs through you.

  1. Send me the job.

    Text or call and tell me what kind of business it is. I create a questionnaire for that type of business.

  2. Your client answers the questionnaire.

    I send you the questionnaire document to pass along. It takes about 20 minutes, asks what a good copywriter would ask, and comes back with their photos.

  3. You review the finished site.

    Within 24 hours of the questionnaire coming back: every page written fresh from the answers, their photos in place, built mobile-first and checked page by page.

  4. Your client approves. I transfer the site.

    Send me any changes and I make them. Once your client approves and you sign off, I transfer the website to you and I’m out of the picture.
