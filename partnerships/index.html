The engagement model

Three stages, from first conversation to full volume.

  1. Conversation

    We start with your agency’s numbers: how many websites you deliver a month, the kinds of businesses you build for, and whether you deliver in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript or in WordPress. Your partnership pricing is quoted from there.

  2. Paid pilot batch

    A smaller first batch of your client sites runs through the complete process: questionnaires out, each site ready for review within 24 hours of its completed questionnaire, revisions, transfer. Your team judges the quality and the turnaround on real work before volume ramps.

  3. Full volume

    Production ramps to your agency’s full volume. Capacity runs up to 600 websites a month, and the 24-hour review window holds at that volume.

Ownership

White-label

Your name on every site. Ours on none.

  • Your name

    Sites are built under your agency’s name. Duna Marketing appears nowhere on them.

  • Your clients

    We never contact them. The questionnaire and every change run through your team.

  • Your sites

    Once your client approves and your team signs off, we transfer the site to your agency.

  • Your hosting

    We don’t host. You host each site wherever you like, and any developer can edit it.

Pricing

Volume-based partnership pricing.

Pricing scales with the number of websites your agency delivers each month, and it’s quoted for each agency. Prices aren’t published; your quote follows the partnership conversation.

Scope

What we build.

Complete multipage websites for local and small businesses of any kind, from a law practice to a ski shop. Online stores and web applications are outside our scope.
