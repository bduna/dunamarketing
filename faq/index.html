Questions

If yours isn’t here, text me. That’s faster than reading anyway.

Price and payment

How much does it cost?

Less than you’re expecting. If you’ve been quoted $1,500 to $3,000 for a website, text me before you sign. I’ll give you my number straight away, along with answers to anything else you want to know.

When do I pay?

Half to start the build, half when you approve the preview. Nothing goes live until you’ve said yes.

Is anything extra?

No. Design, words, photos, a contact form or booking link, and hosting are all in the one price. The only thing you bring is your domain name.

Do I keep paying for hosting?

No. Hosting is included, and there’s no monthly fee.

The process

How fast is “under 24 hours”?

The clock starts when your questionnaire comes in. Your preview link arrives within 24 hours of that.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing before you text. The questionnaire takes about 20 minutes and you can answer it out loud on your phone. If you have photos or a logo, send them along. If you don’t, I’ll source the photos.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You answer questions. I do the rest, including putting the site online.

What if I want changes?

You see a preview first. Two rounds of changes are included, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

Do I have to talk to a salesperson?

No. You text me, and I’m the person who builds the site.

Your site

Will it be on my own domain?

Yes. If you already have one, I’ll need access to where it’s registered so I can point it at the site. If you don’t, buying one takes a few minutes and I’ll walk you through it.

Will it work on phones?

It’s built for a phone first, then desktop.

How many pages do I get?

A full site with real pages, like the samples: home, services, about, contact, and whatever else your business needs.

Can customers contact me through the site?

Yes. A contact form or a booking link is included.

The samples

Are the samples real businesses?

No. They’re mock businesses I built so you can see the design without anyone’s real details. Every one was built the same way yours will be.

Is any of your work live?

Yes. Deep Roots Hydroponics, a grow shop in Sebastopol, CA, is live at deeproots707.com. It’s on the samples page.
