Questions

Everything people ask before they get in touch.

If yours isn’t here, text or call me. That is faster than reading anyway.

What it costs and how it is paid

How much does it cost?

Text or call me and Barrett will tell you straight away, along with answers to anything else you want to know. Whatever your site needs is in one quote: design, words, photos, a contact form and hosting.

When do I pay?

Half to start the build, half when you approve the preview. Nothing goes live until you have said yes.

Is anything extra?

No. Design, words, photos, a contact form or booking link and hosting are all part of the build. The only thing you bring is your domain name.

Do I keep paying for hosting?

No. Hosting is part of the build and I look after it.

The process

How fast is “under 24 hours”?

The clock starts when your questionnaire comes in. Your preview link arrives within 24 hours of that.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing before you get in touch. The questionnaire is a document you fill in, and it takes about 20 minutes. If you have photos or a logo, send them along. If you don’t, I’ll source the photos.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You answer questions. I do the rest, including putting the site online.

What if I want changes?

You see a preview first. Two rounds of changes are included, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

Do I have to talk to a salesperson?

No. You reach Barrett, and I am the person who builds the site.

Your site

Will it be on my own domain?

Yes. If you already have one, I’ll need access to where it’s registered so I can point it at the site. If you don’t, buying one takes a few minutes and I’ll walk you through it.

Will it work on phones?

It is built for a phone first, then desktop.

How many pages do I get?

A full site with real pages, like the samples: home, services, about, contact, and whatever else your business needs.

Can customers contact me through the site?

Yes. A contact form or a booking link is included.

The samples

Are the samples real businesses?

No. They are mock businesses I built so you can see the design without anyone’s real details. Every one was built the same way yours will be.

Is any of your work live?

Yes. Deep Roots Hydroponics, a grow shop in Sebastopol, California, is live at deeproots707.com. It is on the work page.
