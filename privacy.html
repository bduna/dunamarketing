Privacy policy

This page explains what this website collects and what we do with it. Effective September 15, 2026.

When you text, call, or email us

We receive whatever you send: your phone number or email address, your name if you give it, and what you tell us about your business. We use it to send you the questionnaire, to build and deliver your website, and to follow up about it. It's kept in our phone, email, and customer records.

The Meta pixel

This site uses the Meta (Facebook) pixel so we can tell whether our ads are working. It sets cookies and reports page views, and taps on the text, call, and email links, to Meta. You can limit this in your browser settings, through your Meta ad preferences, or with an ad blocker. We do not receive your name from the pixel.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services we need to run this site and deliver your website.
