6 · The review gap — the one thing content can't fix

Zero reviews against a local median of twelve is the biggest single factor holding this profile back — and no rewrite fixes it. What fixes it: asking. Past clients who said things like “we're really glad we worked with someone we could trust” will usually say it on Google if asked once, politely, with the direct review link in their hand. We recommend texting the five most recent happy clients that link this week.

We won't promise a map ranking — nobody honestly can. Review count, review pace and proximity dominate that. What this pack does is make sure that when someone does find the profile, nothing about it loses them.
