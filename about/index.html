Leadership

Barrett Duna, Founder & CEO

Barrett Duna founded Duna Marketing and designed the AI-powered production system it runs on. He has been building websites since 2010: WordPress sites, both coded from scratch and on page builders, web applications in Python and Django, and years of machine-learning work.

His training is in mathematics and economics. He earned a BS in Mathematics/Economics from UCLA in 2013, with his electives focused on data analytics and AI, and took three UCLA Ph.D. courses: Statistical Pattern Recognition (AI), Econometrics and Statistical Surveying.

As a research assistant to UCLA Anderson professor Phillip Leslie, he spent two years turning a 30-page mathematical paper into a working Python simulation of the primary and secondary ticket markets. That work led to a data analyst role at Live Nation Entertainment, where he analyzed millions of ticket transactions to forecast concert ticket sales, study dynamic pricing and understand ticket broker behavior.

He also developed and published a novel AI classification algorithm based on Newton’s law of universal gravitation. He leads Duna Marketing from the San Francisco Bay Area.

Recommendations from his UCLA professors, his manager at Live Nation and his business mentor are at BarrettDuna.com.
