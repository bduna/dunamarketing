About the salon

Known for

Gilded Shears opened in Scottsdale as a reaction to years of rushed, noisy salons — the goal from day one was a room where a color appointment felt like an afternoon off, not an errand.

Color that grows out beautifully, and stylists who actually listen.

Fifteen years behind the chair taught our founder one thing above all: a good salon feels calm, not chaotic. That's the room we built.

Cut and style starts at $75, color from $120, and balayage from $185 — every service is priced to be worth the chair time, not rushed to fit a schedule.
