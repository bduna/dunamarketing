How it works

What booking a detail actually looks like.

  1. Tell us the car

    Call with the year, the make, and what’s bothering you about it — swirls, a wrecked interior, headlights you can’t see through. We’ll say which package fits and what it costs before you commit to anything.

  2. We prep, then we polish

    The car gets washed and decontaminated first, then the paint gets corrected. Nothing goes on top of a panel until what’s underneath it is sorted out.

  3. You pick it up better than you left it

    Interior cleaned and dressed, glass done last so nothing settles on it, and a walk-through of what we found on the way — including anything worth watching before your next visit.

The ceramic coating difference

Gloss that lasts. Water that rolls off.

Ceramic coatings are what we’re known for. A properly prepped and coated finish stays slicker, brighter, and easier to keep clean — season after season.

The prep is most of that job. Paint is decontaminated and corrected before anything gets sealed on top, because a coating locks in whatever is already on the panel, swirls included. It is the slow part of the week and the reason a coated car still looks right long after a wax would have given up.

Before you book

Questions we get on the phone.

What’s the difference between the express wash and the full detail?

The $45 express wash gets the car clean: a careful hand wash and dry, done properly, no machine tunnel. The $180 full detail is the one that changes how the car looks — interior deep-cleaned and dressed, paint decontaminated and polished, glass and trim brought back. If the car has never been detailed, start there.

Is a ceramic coating really worth $600?

It depends how long you’re keeping the car. A coating doesn’t make paint bulletproof — it makes it slicker, so dirt and water let go instead of baking on, and washing gets a lot faster. If the car is staying in your driveway for years and you hate scrubbing it, it earns its money back. If you’re selling in a few months, book the full detail instead and keep the difference.

How long will you have my car?

That depends on the package and the state of the paint — a wash and a coating are not the same afternoon. Call with the vehicle and we’ll give you a real window instead of a guess, and we’ll tell you up front if it needs to stay longer.

Do I need to empty the car first?

Take out anything you don’t want moved — child seats, gym bags, work gear, the stuff in the glovebox. Everything else we’ll work around.

What kind of vehicles do you take?

Daily drivers, trucks, SUVs and weekend cars. The process doesn’t change with the badge on the hood — only how long it takes and, for a coating, how much correction the paint needs first.
