About the shop

From a driveway to a full shop.

I’m obsessive about cars. I started detailing out of my driveway in 2019 and now run a full shop. What we’re known for is the ceramic coatings — and treating every car like it’s my own.

The obsession started the way it usually does: one car, too many hours, and the realisation that the difference between a clean car and a properly finished one is almost entirely patience. Coatings pulled me in because they punish shortcuts — whatever you leave in the paint, you seal in for good.

That’s the whole idea behind Mirror Finish: showroom-new results for the people who actually care how their car looks.

How we work

Three things that don’t change.

  1. Prep before product

    Paint gets washed, decontaminated and corrected before anything is sealed on top of it. Skip that and you have just made the swirls permanent and shiny.

  2. Your car, treated like mine

    A work truck gets the same process as a weekend car. The time it takes changes with the condition of the paint, not with the badge on the hood.

  3. Straight answers on the phone

    If a coating isn’t the right call for how long you’re keeping the car, you’ll hear that before you book rather than after you pay.
