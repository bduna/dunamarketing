Why Mirror Finish

The prep is the part you’re paying for.

Anyone can make a car look good for a weekend. The finish that still looks right months later comes from the unglamorous half of the job — washing, decontaminating and correcting the paint before a drop of product goes on top of it.

That is why the coatings are what we’re known for, and why a work truck gets the same process as a weekend car. Obsessive is just how I do it. It started in a driveway in 2019 and it didn’t change when the shop opened.
