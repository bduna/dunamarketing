About

Who’s behind the counter

Three generations, one city.

Third generation. My grandfather cut meat in this city for forty years, my dad for thirty-five, and I’ve been behind this case since I was nineteen.

That is the whole of it. We know what cut you actually want for what you’re cooking, and we’ll tell you if it’s the wrong one — including when the cut you came in for isn’t the one your dinner needs.

Dry-aged ribeye runs $34, house sausage is $12, ground beef is $8 a pound, and a whole chicken is $22. Whole-animal orders are quoted once we know what you need.
