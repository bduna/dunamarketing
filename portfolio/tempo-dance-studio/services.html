Starting as an adult

Drop-in means exactly that: pay $22 for the class you take, and come back when your week allows. No term to join and nothing to catch up on.

Plenty of adults take their first ballet class here. Stand at the back for the first few if you'd rather — the corrections reach there too.

It ends up on a stage

There's a recital every year, and a competition team you can audition for. Everything we drill in class is pointed at doing it properly when it counts.
