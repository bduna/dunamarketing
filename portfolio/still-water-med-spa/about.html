Our story

“I’m a nurse practitioner who wanted a med spa that felt like a retreat, not a clinic.”

That’s where Still Water started, and it’s still the whole point. We’re known for natural-looking results, and for never pushing treatments you don’t need. Every visit begins with a real conversation about what you’re hoping for — and an honest answer about what will actually help.

The goal is simple: you leave looking refreshed and rested, like yourself on a good day.
