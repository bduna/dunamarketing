Our studio

Everyone belongs on the mat.

We opened Lotus Flow to build a studio where everyone — every body, every level — feels welcome on the mat.

That sentence isn’t a slogan; it’s the founding decision. Everything about the studio follows from it — how classes are taught, how teachers speak to the room, how the phone gets answered when a nervous first-timer calls.

The result is a calm, unpretentious room in Boulder where you can breathe, move, and start — whether it’s your first class or your five-hundredth.

What we believe

Meet yourself where you are.

Yoga isn’t about touching your toes or holding the hardest pose in the room. It’s about showing up, breathing, and giving yourself an hour that’s only yours.

Whether it’s your first class or your five-hundredth, you’ll find teachers who offer options for every body and never rush you. Come once a week or every day — there’s no wrong way to practice here.

How that shows up

Three things we protect.

Unpretentious, on purpose

No posturing, no performance, no expectation that you look a certain way or own the right leggings. The practice is the point.

Teachers who meet you where you are

Every pose comes with options. Your teacher’s job is to find the version that serves you today — not to push you toward someone else’s.

Open every day, 6am to 8pm

Early practice before work, a lunchtime hour, a slow Sunday reset — the room is open daily, so your practice can fit your actual life.

A calm room in Boulder, waiting when you’re ready.

Ready when you are

Come see the room before you commit.
