Your first class

Nervous? Everyone is, on day one.

Here’s exactly what your first visit looks like — what to bring, what happens in the room, and what nobody expects you to be able to do.

What to expect

The whole thing, start to finish.

  1. Before class

    Wear anything you can move in — leggings, sweatpants, an old T-shirt all count. Bring water, skip the heavy meal, and aim to arrive ten minutes early so you can say hello and settle in without rushing.

  2. During class

    Your teacher offers options for every pose — a gentler version and a further one — and you choose, every time. Resting is always allowed, and nobody minds a wobble; the room has seen thousands of them.

  3. After class

    There’s no test and no debrief — you roll up your mat and you’re done. Most people are surprised how quickly the hour goes. If questions came up, your teacher will still be in the room; ask anything.

Common questions

Things first-timers ask us.

Do I need to be flexible to start?

No — this is the biggest myth in yoga. Flexibility is something a practice builds; it isn’t an entry requirement. Come exactly as bendy as you are.

What should I wear and bring?

Anything you can move comfortably in, plus a bottle of water. There’s no dress code. If you don’t own a mat yet, mention it when you book your first class — don’t let gear be the reason you don’t start.

Which class should I start with?

Any of them — every class on the schedule welcomes every level. If you’d like a recommendation, email us or call (303) 555-0319 and tell us what you’re looking for; we’ll point you at a class and a teacher to start with.

What if I need to rest mid-class?

Then rest — genuinely. Taking a break is part of the practice, not a failure of it. Teachers cue resting positions throughout, and nobody will single you out.

How do I sign up?

Email breathe@lotusflowboulder.com or call (303) 555-0319. There’s no app and no account — a person books you in, and your first two weeks are $40, unlimited.

What does it cost after the intro?

Drop-in classes are $20, unlimited months are $140, and private sessions start at $90. The full breakdown is on the classes & pricing page.
