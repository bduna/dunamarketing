Welcome

Come exactly as you are.

Lotus Flow is built around one promise: every body and every level is welcome on the mat.

That means unpretentious classes, teachers who meet you where you are, and a room where nobody is sizing anybody up. If you’re nervous, you’re in good company — most people are on day one, and our teachers plan for it.

You won’t be asked to keep up. You’ll be shown options for every pose and left free to take the gentle one every single time.

How it works

Getting started is three small steps.

  1. Say hello

    Email or call and tell us you’re coming. No account to create, no app to download — a person answers and books you in.

  2. Take any class

    Your $40 intro covers two weeks of unlimited classes. Every class on the schedule welcomes beginners, so pick whatever fits your week.

  3. Find your rhythm

    After two weeks you’ll know which classes and teachers you love. Drop in for $20 when it suits you, or go unlimited at $140 a month.
