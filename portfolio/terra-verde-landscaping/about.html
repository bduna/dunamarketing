About Terra Verde

Fifteen years of Portland yards.

Terra Verde started as a one-man mowing operation in 2009. Today we design and build the kind of gardens people actually live in — the patio you eat dinner on, the lawn the kids run across, the beds that look good with a little water and a little time.

We keep it drought-smart so it stays lush, and our crews clean up after themselves. When we leave, it’s your yard again.

A design consultation is $150 flat. Monthly maintenance starts at $180, and full installs or irrigation are quoted once we’ve seen the yard.
