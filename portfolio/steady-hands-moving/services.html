How the estimate works

Four things worth knowing first.

We would rather over-explain now than argue later. If something here is not clear, ask us on the phone.

  • The estimate happens in person. We come to you, look at the actual stairs and the actual sofa, and price that.
  • Hourly means hourly. Local moves start at $140 an hour for two movers; packing is $65 an hour.
  • Long distance, pianos and safes are quoted. No hourly guessing on the jobs where guessing goes wrong.
  • Wrapping is part of the move. Anything that can be wrapped gets wrapped before it goes near the truck.
