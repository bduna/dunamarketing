About

Who shows up

Two movers, one rented truck, 2014.

We both worked for a company that broke people's things, and we got tired of it. So we rented a box truck and started doing it the other way: fewer moves in a day, everything padded and wrapped, and a real time window instead of “sometime Tuesday”.

Most of our work now comes from people whose friends we already moved — which is the only marketing we have ever been any good at.

Local moves start at $140, packing service from $65, and long distance or safe moving are quoted after the free in-home estimate.
