Thirty minutes, on purpose

Harbor Point Veterinary is a small-animal practice in Annapolis, Maryland.

Why this practice exists

I’ve been a vet for nineteen years. I opened Harbor Point in 2016 so I could spend thirty minutes with a patient instead of twelve.

We get down on the floor with the nervous ones, and we call you with results ourselves. Both of those started as complaints about other clinics — here they are simply how the day runs.

The difference

What eighteen extra minutes buy

A slower hello

A frightened animal gets time to settle before anything is asked of it. Rushing that step is how twelve-minute clinics stay on schedule.

Your questions, answered

The appointment is not over when the exam is. Whatever you came in wondering, there is room left to ask it.

Nothing missed for speed

A proper look at eyes, ears, teeth, skin and gait — the checks that get skipped when the next room is already waiting.

The week here

Saturday mornings exist for a reason

Weekdays run 8am to 6pm, with same-day sick visits for the animals that cannot wait. Saturday mornings, 9am to 1pm, catch everything the work week pushed aside — the vaccine that came due, the lump you have been watching, the check-up you kept meaning to book.
