How it started

I played bars and weddings for fifteen years. I started teaching out of my living room, and it turned out I liked that more than the gigs.

So that's what Fret & Key is: guitar and piano lessons, 30 or 60 minutes, for a parent whose kid asked for guitar lessons and for the forty-year-old who always meant to learn piano. Both of them get the same first lesson, free.

What we're known for is simple enough to say in one line: students actually practice, because we teach songs they want to play from week one. Reading and technique arrive in the middle of a song someone already likes — which is the only version of practice anybody keeps up.

Twice a year everyone plays a recital. It's included, there's no audition, and it gives people a date to aim at.

— the teacher, Fret & Key Music School

Lessons run in half-hour and hour blocks, with practice logs parents can check in on between sessions.
