Why it works

Nobody has to nag anybody

Students actually practice, because we teach songs they want to play from week one.

Reading and technique come along the way, in the middle of a song someone already cares about. That is the whole method, and it is why the guitar comes out of the case between lessons.

Guitar and piano, 30 or 60 minutes, beginners very welcome.

Twice a year, everybody plays

Recitals are included — no extra fee, no audition. You get a date to aim at, which turns out to be the best practice motivator there is.
