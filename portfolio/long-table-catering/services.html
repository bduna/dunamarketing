Cooked where you eat

The kitchen comes to the table.

Everything is cooked on site — we bring our own fire, and the dishes that suffer from sitting are the ones finished last. That is the difference between food that tastes like a restaurant and food that tastes like it waited in a hot box for two hours.

Before service we walk the room, so the food travels the shortest possible distance to the furthest table. It is not glamorous. It is why the short rib gets texted about.

02

On the day, the kitchen arrives, sets up out of sight, and cooks where you eat. If the bar is running, it is stocked and staffed alongside, priced to your evening.
