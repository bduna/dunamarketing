Why every line says “from”

Three things move the number.

A per-person price is a starting point, not a dodge. These are the only things that change it.

  1. Headcount

    Forty people and a hundred and forty are different jobs. More guests usually means a lower number per head, not a higher one.

  2. How it is served

    Family-style platters need fewer hands than a plated dinner with a synchronised drop. Passed canapés need more.

  3. What the market is doing

    We write dishes close to the date, around what is genuinely good that week. That is the point of the whole exercise, and it moves the food cost either way.
