Who calls us

Nervous parents, and grown-up beginners

If your fifteen-year-old is ready and you aren't

The part you're dreading — a fifteen-year-old and a highway on-ramp — is the part you can hand over. The teen package is 30 classroom hours and 6 drive hours for $520, and not one of them is yours to teach.

If you never learned, and you're past the age where people ask

Plenty of people get to thirty, or forty, without a license. Nobody here will make a thing of it. Adult refresher lessons are $85, and single drive lessons are $75.

How the lessons actually run.

Every drive starts somewhere quiet and works up. A first lesson might never leave a car park, and that is fine — there is no schedule to beat and no instructor sighing in the passenger seat. Nervous drivers make faster progress when nobody is rushing them.

Parents are welcome to sit in on a lesson if that helps, and plenty do for the first one. Adults coming back to driving after years away get the same treatment: we start from what you actually remember, not from what the licence says you once knew.
