Booking a first lesson.

Call and tell us who is learning, how much driving they have done, and whether there is a test date already in the diary. That is enough for us to say which package fits and how many hours it realistically takes.

Full teen packages cover 30 classroom hours and 6 hours behind the wheel. Single lessons and adult refreshers are booked one at a time, and our cars can be rented for the road test itself if yours is not suitable.

We answer the phone during business hours; outside them, leave a message with a callback number and we will ring you the next working morning.
