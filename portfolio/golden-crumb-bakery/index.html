Custom cakes

Need a cake by Saturday? Call and ask.

Most of the cakes that leave here are birthday cakes for the weekend, ordered by phone the week before. The most-ordered is the naked lemon-berry cake — layers you can see, fruit on top, nothing that tastes like it came from a grocery store. Custom cakes start at $75 and go up with the design.

About the shop

It started on a porch.

Honestly, it started as a pandemic thing: Dana baking sourdough in her kitchen and selling it off the porch until it got out of hand. In 2021 she and her sister Jess opened the shop on Kentucky Street, just the two of them. Now there are five of us and two ovens, and we still remember people’s names.

People say the sourdough is the best in Sonoma County. Some of them drive up from Novato every Saturday to prove it.
