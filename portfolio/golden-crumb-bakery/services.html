The daily bake

Out of the oven by seven.

The sourdough is a two-day loaf — shaped the night before, baked while Petaluma is still dark, and still warm when the doors open at seven. Nine dollars, and the Saturday bake is the one people cross town for.

Pastries run $2–5 and follow the seasons: croissants every day, apple hand pies when fall arrives, and the Saturday cinnamon rolls — gone by ten, with a reliability we’ve stopped apologizing for.

Everything in the case was baked for that morning. When it’s empty, that’s the day — most days, that’s early afternoon.

Custom cakes

Cakes that taste as good as they look.

Birthdays, showers, whatever you’re celebrating — from $75. Tell us the theme, the flavor, and how many people it needs to feed. That’s everything we need to give you a number on the phone.

If Saturday’s possible, we’ll say so. If it’s not, we’ll say that too.

Wedding cakes

Tiers, tastings, delivery.

Wedding cakes are the one thing we slow down for. You come in and taste the actual cake — not a description of it — and we design the tiers around your date and your headcount.

Dana bakes and stacks every tier herself, and we deliver and set up — a wedding cake’s last twenty minutes of travel are not the moment for improvising. Quoted by phone once we know the date.

Wholesale

The bread that leaves by the back door.

Two cafés in town open with our bread every morning, and the sandwich shop next door slices its country loaf before we’ve swept the floor. That loaf earns its own rack in the second oven.

There’s room for more. If you run a kitchen, call with your volume and we’ll tell you honestly whether two ovens can carry it.
