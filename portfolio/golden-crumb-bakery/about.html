About

It started on a porch.

Honestly, it started as a pandemic thing. Dana was baking sourdough in her kitchen and selling it off the porch, and it kind of got out of hand.

In 2021 she and her sister Jess opened the shop on Kentucky Street. Now there are five of us and two ovens. People say it’s the best sourdough in Sonoma County — we don’t know about that, but we’ll take it.

What we do know: the Saturday cinnamon rolls are gone by ten, the sourdough is still warm at open, and we remember people’s names.
