Dental anxiety

We know what people dread about this. So we deal with it first.

A lot of dental anxiety isn’t really about pain. It’s about not knowing: how long you’ll sit in the waiting room, what the bill will be, whether you’re about to be talked into something. Those are the parts of a visit we can control, so we do.

  1. You’ll know the price before you sit down.

    Every routine service is printed with a number beside it. A first visit with a full exam and x-rays is $89, a cleaning is $120, whitening is $350, an Invisalign consultation is free. The one thing we can’t price until we’ve looked — a crown — says so on the list. Nobody gets a surprise at the front desk.

  2. We run on time.

    The waiting room is where dread does its work, so we don’t leave you in it. Running on time is one of the two things this practice is known for, and it is not a small thing when your stomach is already in a knot before you’ve parked the car.

  3. We tell you what can wait.

    We never sell you a procedure you don’t need. If a filling can safely wait a year, you’ll hear exactly that, and the decision stays yours. It is the other thing we’re known for, and it is the reason anxious patients come back for the second visit.

  4. Patience is part of the appointment.

    Nervous patients don’t get hurried. Tell us when you book and again when you arrive, and we’ll go at your pace — explaining what’s about to happen, pausing when you need it, picking up when you’re ready. It’s why one patient’s first cleaning in six years happened here and not somewhere else.

Your first visit

Here is exactly what happens.

  1. Call, and say you’re nervous.

    That is useful information, not an inconvenience. Say how long it’s been, too. The front desk has been here longer than the current owner and has heard every version of it. We open at 7am, so an appointment before work is possible.

  2. A full exam and x-rays, $89.

    Your first appointment is a look and a conversation: a full examination, a set of x-rays on the newer machine, and time for your questions. You’ll know the price before you come in, and it won’t change when you get there.

  3. A plan with the prices on it.

    If anything needs attention, you’ll hear what, why, what it costs, and what can wait. Crowns are the one thing we quote after we’ve looked. Then you decide — nothing gets scheduled that you haven’t agreed to.

Questions

What nervous patients ask before they book.

It’s been years since I saw a dentist. Is that a problem?

No. Long gaps are common here, and nobody delivers a lecture about them. One of our reviews is from a patient whose first cleaning in six years was with us. The first visit is an exam and x-rays for $89; we look, we talk, and you decide what happens next.

Will I be talked into treatment I don’t need?

We never sell you a procedure you don’t need. If something can wait, we say so — one patient wrote that we told him a filling could wait a year instead of doing it that day. You’ll hear what needs doing, what can wait, and what each costs, and the decision is yours.

How long will I be sitting in the waiting room?

Not long. We run on time; it’s one of the two things this practice is known for. Your appointment starts when it was booked to start, so there is no long stretch in the waiting room for the nerves to build.

What will it cost?

A new patient exam with x-rays is $89. Cleanings are $120, whitening is $350, an Invisalign consultation is free, and crowns are quoted after we’ve examined the tooth. The full list explains each one.

Is this a new practice?

No — it has thirty years behind it. Dr. Whitcomb ran it for three decades; the current owner bought it in 2018 and kept the chairs and the front desk staff, and replaced the x-ray machine. The people who answer the phone are the same people who did before.

When can I come in?

Monday to Thursday from 7am to 5pm, and Friday from 7am to 2pm. Because we open at 7, an appointment before work is possible. Call (425) 555-0410 or email smile@cedarhilldental.com.
