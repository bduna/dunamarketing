Thirty years, then us

The practice did not start in 2018. That is only when it changed hands.

In the owner’s words

“I bought this practice from Dr. Whitcomb in 2018 after he’d run it for thirty years. Same chairs, same front desk staff, better x-ray machine.”

The people at the front desk are the ones who were already here. The chairs are the ones Dr. Whitcomb chose. What we replaced was the x-ray machine.

We are a family dental practice in Bellevue, WA, and the two things we are known for are short: we run on time, and we never sell you a procedure you don’t need.
