About the practice

Thirty years, then us.

The practice didn’t start in 2018. That is just when it changed hands. Here is what changed, what didn’t, and why nervous patients do well here.

The owner

Same chairs. Same front desk. Better x-ray machine.

“I bought this practice from Dr. Whitcomb in 2018 after he’d run it for thirty years. Same chairs, same front desk staff, better x-ray machine.”

That is the whole story, and it is a more reassuring one than it sounds. Dr. Whitcomb ran this practice for thirty years. When the current owner bought it in 2018, the people at the front desk stayed, the chairs stayed, and the one thing that got replaced was the x-ray machine.

If you are anxious about the dentist, familiar matters. The voice on the phone has been answering it for years. The room is a working practice, not a showroom. And the one piece of equipment that changed is the one that lets us see what is going on instead of guessing.

What we’re known for

Two things, and what they mean for you.

We run on time.

An appointment at 8:00 starts at 8:00. That sounds like a small courtesy until you are the person in the waiting room with a knot in your stomach, watching the clock and rehearsing reasons to leave. We don’t give you that time, because we know what you would do with it.

It also means an appointment before work is a real option, not a gamble. We open at 7am Monday to Friday.

We never sell you a procedure you don’t need.

If a filling can wait a year, we say so and let you decide. If a crown is the right call, we explain why and quote it before anything is scheduled. One patient’s review asks “who does that?” We do.

For an anxious patient this is the promise that matters most: you will never be talked into something in the chair. You’ll be told what needs doing, what can wait, and what each costs, and then it is your call.

The front desk

The first people you’ll talk to have been here longer than we have.

The front desk staff came with the practice, and they have been answering this phone since before 2018. Tell them you are nervous. Tell them how long it has been. They’ll book the appointment, and it won’t be the first time they have heard either.

They are also the ones who will tell you what your visit costs before you come in. The price list isn’t a secret, and neither is the schedule.
