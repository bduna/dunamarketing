When you call

Four things worth saying on the phone.

  • That you’re nervous.It helps us, and the front desk has heard it many times. Nobody will make a thing of it.
  • How long it’s been.Five years, ten, longer — to us it is a scheduling detail, not a confession. The first visit is the same $89 exam and x-rays either way.
  • What you want to know the price of.Ask. Everything routine has a number, and we’ll tell you before you come in, so there is nothing to brace for at the desk.
  • Whether mornings are easier.We open at 7am, Monday to Friday. If getting it done before work makes it easier to actually come, say so and we’ll find the slot.
