If you’re nervous

What every one of these visits has in common.

Whatever you’re in for, the shape of the appointment is the same, because the things that make people anxious don’t change from one service to the next.

  • It starts on time.You are not left in the waiting room working up to it. Running on time is one of the two things this practice is known for.
  • You hear what’s happening before it happens.The x-ray goes up on the screen and we walk you through it. Questions are welcome at any point, and “can this wait?” is a good one.
  • Nothing is added on.We never sell you a procedure you don’t need. If we recommend something, you’ll hear why, what it costs, and what happens if you wait.
  • You can take it slowly.Say you’re anxious when you book and again when you arrive. We’ll go at your pace; we have done this with a lot of people.
