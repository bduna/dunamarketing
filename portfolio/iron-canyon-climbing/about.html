How it started

A decade in the canyons, then a gym

Two of us bolted routes in the Wasatch for a decade before building this place in 2019. Forty-foot walls, four hundred routes, reset weekly.

Setting is the part we came from, so it is the part that sets the schedule: routes that get reset often enough that regulars never get bored.

If you tried climbing once on a trip and want to actually start, the intro class is $45 and the gear comes with it.
