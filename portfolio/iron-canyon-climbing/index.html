Who runs this place

Two route-setters from the Wasatch

Two of us bolted routes in the Wasatch for a decade before building this place in 2019. Forty-foot walls, four hundred routes, reset weekly.

That last part is the whole idea: routes that get reset often enough that regulars never get bored.
