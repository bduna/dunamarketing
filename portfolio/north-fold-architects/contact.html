Before the first meeting.

The useful things to have are the address, a rough budget, and some idea of how you want to live in the house — where you eat, whether you work from home, how many people are in it in ten years. Plans can follow from that; they cannot lead it.

If planning permission is likely to be difficult, it is better to know that at the first conversation than after drawings exist. We will tell you plainly if a site looks like a fight.
