Why only four a year.

A house takes the time it takes. Taking on more work than that means drawings get delegated, site visits get skipped, and the person you hired stops being the person doing the thinking. Four projects is the number where that does not happen.

It also means the site gets walked properly before anything is drawn — where the wind comes from, where the light lands in winter, which trees are worth keeping. That is an afternoon that saves a year of regret.
