Before you come

A few things worth knowing.

Lunch runs hard between noon and one, and the line moves faster than it looks. If you are feeding a group, phoning ahead is the difference between waiting fifteen minutes and picking up a bag that is already packed.

Family platters start at $28 and need a little lead time on a busy day. Catering is quoted once we know the date and the headcount — give us as much notice as you can and the trompo comes to you.

Closed Mondays, every week. Tuesday through Sunday we are open 11am to 9pm, and we stay until the al pastor is gone.
