The rest of the board

Everything else we make.

Tacos are $3.50 each, so most people order three and a drink. Burritos are $9 and come the way they should — rice, beans, your meat, wrapped tight enough to eat standing up. Quesadillas are $8. Family platters start at $28 and feed four people who are actually hungry, which is the order to place when you are taking dinner home instead of eating at the truck.

Catering is quoted by the head once we know the date and the count. We have done backyard parties, office lunches and a wedding rehearsal — the trompo travels.

The salsa is the other thing people ask about. It is made fresh every morning, and enough customers have asked to buy it by the jar that we started keeping some behind the counter.
