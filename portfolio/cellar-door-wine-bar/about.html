About

A former bakery basement, since 2019.

Cellar Door is a wine bar in Providence, Rhode Island — small producers, candlelight, old brick, and nobody looking at the clock.

The short version

I got tired of watching good bottles get ruined.

I sold wine wholesale for a decade and got tired of watching restaurants ruin good bottles.

So in 2019 I opened Cellar Door in a former bakery basement: a list of small producers you won’t find in a grocery store, and staff who ask what you actually like before they pour you anything.

That’s still the whole idea. Come at four or come at eleven — we’re open until midnight either way. Ask for “something like a Barolo, but cheaper” and you’ll get an answer, not a lecture.
