Why there’s a wine bar in a basement

Ten years in wholesale, watching good bottles get ruined.

I sold wine wholesale for a decade and got tired of watching restaurants ruin good bottles. So in 2019 I opened Cellar Door in a former bakery basement — a list of small producers you won’t find in a grocery store, and staff who ask what you actually like.

Come at four, stay till midnight. Nobody is going to rush you, and nobody is going to make you feel small for asking a question.
