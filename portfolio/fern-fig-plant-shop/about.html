About

Why we’re here

I killed every plant I owned until I was twenty-eight. Then something clicked.

Fern & Fig opened in 2020 to help people get past that stage faster. We tell you honestly which plants will survive your apartment — and we take questions forever, long after you’ve paid. If something’s struggling, bring it in.

More about the shop →

Nothing here is hard to keep alive.

Every plant on the shelves has been grown on in the shop for a few weeks before it goes out, which is the only honest way to know it has settled. If something is fussy, we say so at the counter rather than letting you find out three weeks later.

Tell us which window it is going in and how often you actually remember to water things. That answer decides the plant far more than what looks best on the shelf.
