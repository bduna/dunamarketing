Bringing a plant in.

If something you already own is struggling, bring it in rather than describing it. Nine times out of ten it is watering or light, and both are easier to diagnose with the plant on the counter and the soil in front of us.

Repotting is done in the shop while you wait when it is straightforward. Larger specimens are worth ringing ahead about so we have the right pot and compost to hand.
