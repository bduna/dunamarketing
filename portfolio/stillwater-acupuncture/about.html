About · Portland, Oregon

Five years in Chengdu, fourteen in practice.

A single-practitioner acupuncture practice in Portland, Oregon, for chronic pain, migraines and insomnia.

Six people a day is the whole idea.

I studied five years in Chengdu and have practiced for fourteen. I treat about six people a day, which is deliberately not many.

Most people who find me have already tried everything else for chronic pain, migraines or sleep. They arrive skeptical, and they are usually right to be — they have been told before that something would work. Skepticism is easier to work with than faith, and it does not change the treatment.

What I am known for, if anything: patience with people who are skeptical, and results for migraines and sleep.
