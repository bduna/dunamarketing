About the practice

Fourteen years downtown, then this.

I spent fourteen years in a large firm’s trusts and estates group — estates with nine figures and family meetings with name cards. It was excellent training and a strange way to live.

What I noticed, year after year, was that the documents doing the most good weren’t the clever ones. They were the ordinary wills that existed — signed, valid, findable — in families that would otherwise have spent a year in court. And the people who most needed them weren’t calling firms like mine, because nobody would tell them what it cost.

So in 2019 I opened this practice in San Diego with the pricing printed and the billable hour left behind. An estate plan is a known amount of work. It should have a known price, and you should understand every page you sign.
