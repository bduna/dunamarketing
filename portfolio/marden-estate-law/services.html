The honest version

Most people need the will, not the trust.

A revocable living trust is a good tool that gets sold to people who don’t need it, because it costs more. The trust package here exists for the cases where it earns its keep — property in more than one state, a child whose inheritance needs managing, an estate where privacy matters.

If your situation is a will-package situation, that is what you’ll be told, and the fee stays at $950. The practice makes less money that way. It also gets to keep its name.

The first meeting needs a rough idea of what you own and who matters to you. The paperwork comes later — that’s the point of doing this properly.
