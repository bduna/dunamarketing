Businesses & safes

Offices, storefronts, and the safe nobody has the combination to.

Commercial master key systems are quoted per building: one key for the doors you choose, separate keys for the doors you don’t. Call with the number of doors and who needs to open what, and you’ll have a number.

Safes are the same. Tell me the make, the size and what kind of lock is on it — dial, keypad, key — and I price the opening on the phone before I drive out. If I can’t price it from your description, I’ll say so.

Questions

What people ask while I’m on the way.

How fast can you get here?

Under thirty minutes across the Denver metro, any hour. Give me the cross streets or the garage level and I’ll tell you on the phone how long it will take from where I am.

Will the price change when you arrive?

No. You hear the price on the phone before I drive out, and that is what you pay. Car lockouts are $85, home lockouts $95, rekeys from $25 a lock, deadbolts from $120. Nothing is added at the door.

Do you really answer at 3am?

Yes, and it is me, not an answering service. That is when people actually need a locksmith, so that is when the phone is on. Call (720) 555-0643.

Are you licensed?

Licensed and bonded, eleven years working the Denver metro.

Should I rekey after buying a house?

Yes. You have no idea how many copies of the old key exist. Rekeying keeps your locks and gives you new keys that the old ones can’t match — from $25 a lock, however many doors you have. One customer did the whole house the afternoon he closed.

What should I have ready when I call?

Where you are (cross streets, garage level, building), what’s locked (car, house, safe), and for a car, the make and colour. That is enough for me to quote it and start driving.

Ready when you are

One call, one price, under thirty minutes.
