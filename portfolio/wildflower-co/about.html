About us

It started at a farmers-market stand.

One of us fell in love with flowers working a farmers-market stand, and it grew from there. Wildflower opened in 2019, and we still buy local when we can — which is part of why our arrangements look loose and garden-grown, not like they came from a grocery-store bucket.
