The curator

A little shop with a big heart.

I curate pieces from Southwestern makers I actually know. Juniper is a little shop with a big heart on Canyon Road.

That's the whole idea, really. Every ceramic, textile, candle, and piece of local art on the shelves comes from someone whose work I trust — handmade goods with a story, in a shop that (I'm told) smells amazing.

Whether you're after a special gift or a beautiful piece for your own home, I'd love to help you find it. And whatever you choose leaves beautifully wrapped — free, always.

— Juniper
