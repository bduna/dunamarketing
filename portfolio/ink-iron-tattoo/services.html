How we work

We say no if it won’t age well.

Four artists, one shop, no walk-in flash. We opened in 2016 because we wanted a place where the work came before the volume — fine-line and blackwork that still reads clean ten years later.

That means the answer is sometimes no: wrong placement, wrong scale, wrong idea for skin that has to carry it for decades. You’ll hear that in the consultation, not after.

“They talked me out of a spot that would’ve blurred. Grateful.”

— Marla K.
