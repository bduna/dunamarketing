Our story

The teacher behind Bright Path.

Bright Path started with a simple idea: the kids who need help most rarely get it in a room of thirty.

I taught high school math for eleven years. Bright Path is my way of giving kids the one-on-one help a classroom of thirty can’t.

In a full classroom, the student who’s quietly falling behind is the easiest one to miss. One-on-one, there’s nowhere to hide and no reason to — we slow down on the parts that are hard, and keep going until they click.

That’s what Bright Path is about: patient tutors, real one-on-one attention, and building confidence — not just grades.

— The team at Bright Path Tutoring
