What “from” means

Where a line says “from”, that is the starting price and the garment decides the rest. Where it does not, the number is the number.

Bringing in something you are worried about? Say so on the phone. We actually get the stain out, and we tell you honestly when we cannot — and we would rather tell you before you have paid for it.

Worth knowing

Three things the list assumes.

One

Same-day is a real option

Anything in before ten in the morning is usually back the same day. After ten, tell us the day you need it and we will say straight away whether we can hit it.

Two

Per piece, not per bag

Every line above is priced by the garment, so a bag of five is five prices you can add up yourself before you hand it over.

Three

$30 is the delivery line

About ten shirts, or a suit and a couple of pieces. Over that, pickup and delivery cost nothing. Under it, the shop is the cheaper trip.

Alterations · from $18

Bring the shoes with the trousers.

Hems, waists, sleeves and zippers. We pin the hem to the shoes you will actually wear with them, on the counter, with you standing there — which is the difference between a trouser length and a guess.

If it needs cleaning as well, we clean it first. It is one trip either way, and no appointment is needed for any of it.
