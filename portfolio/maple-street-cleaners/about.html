About the shop

Two owners, one corner, since 1994.

Who runs Maple Street Cleaners, how a garment is handled once it leaves the counter, and what it all costs.

Who you’re handing it to

Same presses, same neighborhood.

My parents bought this shop in 1994 and I took it over in 2015. Same presses, same neighborhood, same Saturday regulars — a few of whom have been bringing in shirts longer than I’ve been running the place.

What we’re known for is simple enough: we actually get the stain out, and we tell you honestly when we can’t. Nobody likes hearing the second one, but it beats paying for it twice.

That’s most of what the counter conversation is for. Point out the mark, tell us what caused it if you know, say when you need the piece back, and we’ll tell you there and then what we think we can do about it. Same-day is usually possible on anything in before ten.

— Maple Street Cleaners, Cincinnati, Ohio
