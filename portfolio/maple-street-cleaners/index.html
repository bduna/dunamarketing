What we do

Four things, and what each one costs.

Everything is priced per piece, not per bag, so the number you hear at the counter is the number on the ticket when you pick it up. If your piece isn’t obviously one of these four, call and describe it — we’ll tell you which line it falls on.

Shirts, laundered

$3.25 a shirt

Washed, pressed and back on a hanger — folded instead if you say so when you drop them off. Ten shirts is roughly where an order clears $30, which is where pickup and delivery stop costing anything.

Dry cleaning

from $8.50 a piece

Suits, dresses, coats and ties. Point out the stain when you hand it over and tell us what caused it if you know — that is the difference between a guess and a plan, and it costs you nothing to say.

Alterations

from $18

Hems, waists, sleeves and zippers. Bring the shoes you’ll wear with the trousers and we’ll pin the hem to those instead of guessing at it. If it needs cleaning too, we clean it first — it’s one trip either way.

Wedding gown preservation

$310

Cleaned and boxed so it keeps. One flat price whatever the dress, and we’ll walk you through what the box protects against and what it doesn’t before you leave it with us.

Before you drop off

What people ask at the counter.

Four things worth knowing before you hand anything over. Anything else, the phone is quicker than this page.

How fast can I have it back?

Same-day is usually possible on anything in before ten in the morning. After that, tell us the day you need it and we’ll say straight away whether it’s doable rather than promising and calling you later.

What if the stain doesn’t come out?

We’ll say so before we start if we don’t like its chances, and again afterwards if it didn’t lift. Nobody enjoys hearing it, but it beats paying to be disappointed twice.

Hangers or folded?

Shirts come back on hangers unless you ask for them folded. Say which when you drop them off and it goes on your ticket, so you only have to say it once.

Do I need an appointment for alterations?

No — walk in during opening hours and we’ll look at it with you on the counter. Bring the shoes you’ll wear with the trousers so the hem gets pinned to those.
