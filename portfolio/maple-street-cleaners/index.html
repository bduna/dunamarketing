Who walks through the door

Nearly everyone arrives with one of two problems.

One is a deadline. The other is a stain. They need different answers — and you should get yours at the counter, not a week later on the phone.

Problem one

“I need this suit by Thursday.”

A wedding, an interview, a funeral. The date was never negotiable, and the suit has been hanging in the wardrobe since the last one.

What happens: tell us the day when you drop it off. In before ten and it is usually back the same day; if your date is tight we will say yes or no while you are still standing there, rather than promise now and call you later.

Problem two

“I don’t think this is ever coming out.”

Red wine, oil, ink, or something nobody can name. You have probably already tried something at home and you are not sure whether that helped or made it worse.

What happens: point it out and tell us what caused it if you know. We say what we rate its chances at before we start, and we say so again afterwards if it did not lift.

What we do

Five lines. Five numbers. No sixth one at the till.

Everything is priced per piece rather than per bag, so the figure you hear at the counter is the figure on the ticket when you collect. Where a line says “from”, the garment decides the rest — never the day of the week.

Ask us about your stain

Before you drop off

The questions we answer at the counter every day.

Anything not here, the phone is quicker than this page.

How fast can I have it back?

Anything in before ten in the morning is usually back the same day. After ten, tell us the day you need it and we will say straight away whether it is doable, rather than promising now and calling you later.

What if the stain doesn’t come out?

We will say so before we start if we do not rate its chances, and again afterwards if it did not lift. Nobody enjoys hearing it, but it beats paying to be disappointed twice.

Hangers or folded?

Hangers, unless you ask for folded. Say which at drop-off and it goes on your ticket, so you only have to say it once.

Do I need an appointment for alterations?

No. Walk in during opening hours and we will look at it with you on the counter. Bring the shoes you will wear with the trousers so the hem gets pinned to those.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Call and give us a day and a window when someone is home. It is free on orders over $30 — roughly ten shirts, or a suit and a couple of pieces — and under that the shop is the cheaper trip.

Do you take wedding gowns?

Yes. $310 flat, cleaned and boxed for storage, whatever the dress. We explain what the box protects against and what it does not before you leave it with us.
