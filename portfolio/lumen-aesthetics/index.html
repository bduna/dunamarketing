How it works

From first email to a straight answer.

  1. Write or call

    Tell the office what you’ve been thinking about, in your own words. You don’t need to have decided anything, and you don’t need the right terminology — that part is my job.

  2. The consultation

    $200, credited toward surgery if you go ahead. We begin with the thing that brought you in and work out, honestly, what an operation could change, what it couldn’t, and whether you need one at all.

  3. A quote, or a no

    If surgery is the right call, you leave with a specific quote and a plan. If it isn’t, you’ll be told so plainly — and the $200 will have bought you exactly what you came in for.
