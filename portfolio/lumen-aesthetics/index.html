How it works

From first email to a straight answer.

Three steps, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. Write or call

    Tell the office what you have been thinking about, in your own words. You do not need to have decided anything, and you do not need the right terminology — that part is my job.

  2. The consultation

    $200, credited toward surgery if you go ahead. We start with the thing that brought you in and work out what an operation could change, what it could not, and whether you need one at all.

  3. A quote, or a no

    If surgery is the right call you leave with a specific quote and a plan. If it is not, you will be told so plainly, on the day, rather than a week later on the phone.

A word before you book

Who this practice is for.

Not every practice suits every patient, and it is better to say so on a website than in a consultation room. A rough guide:

Probably you, if…

  • You have been reading about this for a long time and want a plain answer about what is realistic before you commit to anything.
  • You would rather look rested than look different, and you would like the people around you not to be able to say why.
  • You want a surgeon who is willing to say no.

Probably not, if…

  • You want the most dramatic change that can be achieved, rather than the smallest one that solves the problem.
  • You would rather be told what to have done than talk it through.
  • You are deciding on price alone. That is a fair way to shop; it just is not what this practice is built around.
