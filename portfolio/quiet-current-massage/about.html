How I got here

Touch was the thing that helped.

I was a hospice nurse for eleven years. Touch was the thing that helped when nothing else could. I trained in massage in 2019 and never went back.

What I carried over from that work is mostly restraint. I don’t fill the hour with talk, I don’t upsell you anything, and I don’t pretend a light rub is deep tissue. You tell me where it hurts and how hard to go, and then the room goes quiet.

Most of the people on my table are carrying a week in their shoulders and want an hour where nobody needs anything from them. That is exactly what this is.
