Six students in a rented rec room

That is how Stone Crane started in 2012. Thirty-one years of training and nineteen of teaching stand behind it.

The instructor

Thirty-one years training, nineteen teaching

I’ve trained for thirty-one years and taught for nineteen. Stone Crane opened in 2012 with six students in a rented rec room.

What we’re known for is discipline without yelling — and belts that mean something, because they take years. Most people who walk in are a parent who wants their kid to gain confidence, or an adult who wants to learn something hard.
