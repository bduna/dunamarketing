How it started

Eleven years of practice, then a loan.

We started as two homebrewers with one garage between us. Eleven years of practice went into it before a small business loan in 2020 made it a real taproom in Bend.

There are ten taps now and six of them are ours. The other four are guest lines. The hazy IPA is the one people drive out here for — if you only have time for one glass, that’s the glass.

Dogs and kids are both welcome in the room. That was the point of opening it.
