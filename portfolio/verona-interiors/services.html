Where every project starts

Two hours in your rooms.

The consultation is $350 and happens in the rooms that have been waiting — not over a mood board. We walk what you have, including the pieces you want to keep, and talk honestly about what the space can do.

You leave the visit with something on paper: what each room needs, what it would cost, and where to begin if the budget says one thing at a time. If two hours is all you ever want from us, that’s a complete purchase, not a teaser.

Room packages & full-home design

Drawings, finishes, and fabric memos.

A room package takes one room from half-finished to finished: layout, finishes and fabrics, chosen against what is already in the room rather than against a showroom. Full-home design plans every room together, so the house reads as one thing instead of six decisions made in six different years.

The four-project cap matters most at this stage. Fabric memos expire, lead times drift, and a studio juggling a dozen houses loses weeks to catching up — yours stays on the desk until it’s done.

Procurement & renovation

The ordering, handled. The build, watched.

Furnishing procurement runs at trade pricing — we place the orders, chase the lead times and receive the deliveries, so a sofa stuck in a warehouse is our problem and not yours.

Renovation coordination is someone keeping the design straight while the work runs: one person who knows where each of the trades stands, and why that wall is the colour it is. Quoted in writing after the consultation.
