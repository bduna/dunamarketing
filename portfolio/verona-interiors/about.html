About the studio

Four projects at a time

Residential interior design in Sacramento, for people who have stopped seeing their own half-finished house.

The short version

Nine years commercial, residential since 2017

I spent nine years in commercial design before going residential in 2017. I work on four projects at a time so nobody waits three weeks for an email.

It is a deliberate limit. Four means the drawings, the samples and the ordering for your house are all moving in the same week, and there is one person who knows where each of them stands.

“Rooms that look collected over years rather than ordered in a weekend.”

How it goes

From stuck to finished

Every project starts the same way, whether it ends up being one room or the whole house.

  1. 01

    Two hours in your rooms

    The consultation, $350. We walk what you have, including the pieces you want to keep.

  2. 02

    A scope and a price

    A room design package from $2,400, or a written quote for full-home design or renovation coordination.

  3. 03

    Drawings and materials

    Layout, finishes and fabric memos, chosen against what is already in the room.

  4. 04

    Ordering and coordination

    Furnishing procurement at trade pricing, and coordination with the renovation while the work runs.
