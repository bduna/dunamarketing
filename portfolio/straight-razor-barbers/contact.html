First time in the shop.

Tell whoever answers what you are after and roughly how you wear it now — that is enough for us to put you with the right barber and set aside the right amount of time. A cut is $38, a cut and beard is $55, a hot-towel straight razor shave is $45, a beard trim on its own is $25, and kids under twelve are $28.

The shave is the one worth booking rather than walking in for. It is a real hot-towel straight razor shave and it takes the chair for a proper stretch of time, so it is worth ringing ahead rather than hoping.

We are open Tuesday to Saturday, nine to seven. If the phone rings out we are almost certainly mid-cut — leave a message and we will call you back between clients.
