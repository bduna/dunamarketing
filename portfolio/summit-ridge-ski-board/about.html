About the shop

Twenty-three seasons, two blocks from the gondola.

Summit Ridge is a walk-in ski and snowboard rental shop in Breckenridge, Colorado. We would rather have your gear standing on the rack than have you standing in a line.

Reserve your gear See the day rates

The owner

We open before the lifts do.

“I’ve been fitting boots in this town for twenty-three seasons. We’re two blocks from the gondola and we open before the lifts do.”

That is the shop in two sentences. A family lands in Breckenridge late; nobody wants to spend the first morning of the trip standing in a rental line. So call ahead, and the gear is ready before you arrive.

Skis, snowboards, demo skis, kids’ packages, tuning and boot fitting. That is the whole shop.

What we are known for

Two things, done properly.

  • Boot fits that don’t hurt on day threeBoot fitting is by appointment. Twenty-three seasons of it, and it is still the part of the job we care most about.
  • Gear ready before you arriveCall the shop with who is coming and what they ride, and the packages are sized and waiting when you walk in.
