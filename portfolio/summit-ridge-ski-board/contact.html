When you call

Have this ready, and the rest is done.

  • Who is comingWho is skiing and who is riding, their sizes, and how many days. That is all it takes to have the packages sized and standing together when you walk in.
  • Arriving late tonight?We open at 7am in season, before the first lift spins, so gear reserved tonight is ready on the way to the hill tomorrow.
  • Booking a boot fittingBoot fitting is by appointment and unhurried. Call the shop and we will put a time aside — the goal is day three without pain.
  • Prefer to just come by?The shop is a walk-in, two blocks from the gondola. Reserving ahead only means the boots are the one thing left to fit.
