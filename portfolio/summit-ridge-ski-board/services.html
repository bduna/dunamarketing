Boot fitting, tuning, demos

The part a price list can’t show.

A rental package is only as good as the boots in it. Boot fitting here is by appointment and unhurried — twenty-three seasons of it behind this counter, with one goal: day three without pain.

Bring your own gear and a tune is $35, edges and wax, done in the shop. And for the day you want to ride something sharper than a rental, the performance demo wall is $68/day.

  • Boot fitting, by appointment. Call the shop and we’ll put a time aside.
  • Tuning, $35. Your own skis or board, edged and waxed.
  • Demo skis, $68/day. The performance wall, by the day.
