What we’re known for

Two things, said plainly.

The first is the sazerac. People argue about it — ours against theirs, ours against the one down the street. Order it and take a side.

The second matters more. If nothing on the list is what you are after, say what you are in the mood for — bitter, stirred, long, no spirit at all — and a bartender will build it for you. The list is short on purpose.

Spirit-free gets the same care and the same glassware, at $12. Nobody is drinking a lemonade apologetically in this room.

The room

We kept the pharmacy’s cabinets.

I tended bar on Chartres Street for eight years. Velvet Fig opened in 2021 in a former pharmacy — the cabinets are original, and they still hold the bottles.

The rest is what the room is for: somewhere dark and unhurried to have a proper drink, five nights a week.

Before you come

Three things worth knowing.

First time? Order the sazerac.

It is the one people argue about, and the fastest way to decide what you think of us.

Not sure? Describe it instead.

Bitter, stirred, long, spirit-free — say the shape of the drink and a bartender will build it.

Six of you? Send the date.

Tables are held for six or more. Fewer than that, walk in — there is usually room at the bar.
