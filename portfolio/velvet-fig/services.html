How to order here

Say bitter, stirred, spirit-free.

The list is short on purpose. If nothing on it is what you’re after, tell the bartender what you’re in the mood for — bitter, stirred, something long, no spirit at all — and they’ll build something to your taste. That’s the thing we’re actually known for.

Spirit-free gets the same care and the same glassware, at $12. Nobody is drinking a lemonade apologetically in this room.

The private room

For a group that wants the door closed.

Behind the back bar there’s a room with a door, the same cocktails, and no one else. It gets quoted rather than priced per head — send the date, the headcount and how long you want the door closed, and we’ll put a number on it.

For parties of six or more out in the main room, call the bar and we’ll hold the table.

Reservations

Six or more? Call the bar.

We hold tables for parties of six or more, and the private room is by quote. The room is open Wednesday through Sunday, from five in the evening until two.
