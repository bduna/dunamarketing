How it started

A pharmacy, and the cabinets that came with it

I tended bar on Chartres Street for eight years. Velvet Fig opened in 2021 in a former pharmacy — the cabinets are original.

They still hold the bottles. The rest is what the room is for: somewhere dark and unhurried to have a proper drink.

What we're known for is a sazerac people argue about, and bartenders who'll build something to your taste.
