Before you come

What people ask on the phone.

Do I need to book?

Only for six or more — those we hold. Fewer than that, walk in. There is usually room at the bar, and the whole point of the room is that you are not being moved along.

Can I get a drink without alcohol?

Yes, and it is on the same list at $12. Same care, same glassware, built to order like everything else. Nobody is drinking a lemonade apologetically in this room.

Is there food?

Bar snacks from $9 — something to eat while you drink rather than dinner. If you want a full meal first, eat and then come.

What if nothing on the list appeals?

Then describe what you are in the mood for. Bitter, stirred, something long, no spirit at all — a bartender will build it, and it costs the same as anything else on the list.

How does the private room work?

It is quoted to your evening rather than priced per head. Send the date, the headcount and how long you want the door closed and we will put a number on it.

When are you open?

Wednesday to Sunday, five in the evening until two. Wednesday to Sunday.
