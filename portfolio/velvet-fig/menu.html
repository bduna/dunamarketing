What things cost

Drinks

Stirred, shaken, or built to your taste.

The list is short on purpose. If nothing on it is what you are after, tell a bartender what you are in the mood for and they will build it.

  • Cocktails

    $15–18

    Including the sazerac people argue about. Bitter, stirred, long, or whatever you describe at the bar — the price does not change because you asked for something off-list.

  • Spirit-free cocktails

    $12

    The same care and the same glassware, without the spirit. Built to order like everything else.
