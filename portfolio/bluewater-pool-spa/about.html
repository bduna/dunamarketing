About

Five routes, one owner still driving

Seven years on someone else’s routes

“I cleaned pools for someone else for seven years before starting Bluewater in 2018. We run five routes and I still do one of them.”

That is the whole reason the schedule holds. Five routes, Monday to Friday, all of them in Scottsdale — close enough together that your day stays your day through an Arizona summer.

  • Founded 2018
  • Five routes
  • Scottsdale only

What we’re known for is showing up the same day every week and sending a photo when we leave, so you know the visit happened without having to be home for it. Every stop includes a free water test, and if a pool has gone green we can usually bring it back to swimmable within four days.
