The short version

Started in 2018 for the scared kids

2018
The year Blue Lane started
Nine
Summers teaching at a city pool before that

I taught lessons at a city pool for nine summers. I started Blue Lane in 2018 because the classes were too big for kids who were actually scared — a child who needs five minutes at the wall doesn’t get five minutes in a group of twelve.

So we built the school around that child. Groups stay small. The wall stays an option. And we get frightened kids in the water without ever forcing them under — that’s the whole method, and it’s the only promise we make.

Nine summers at a public pool teaches you what actually goes wrong — it isn’t the technique, it’s the fear. Blue Lane starts there instead.

What we’re known for

Getting frightened kids in the water

Parents usually find us after somewhere else went badly — a four-year-old who screamed through lessons is the swimmer we know best.

There is no trick to it. Small groups, patience, and a rule nobody breaks: faces go in the water when the swimmer decides they do. Start with the free assessment and tell us exactly what happened last time.
