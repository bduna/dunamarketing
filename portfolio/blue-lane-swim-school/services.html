What every lesson has in common

Nobody gets forced under

It is the one promise we make, and it applies to the four-year-old who screamed somewhere else, the infant in the six-week course, and the adult who never learned. Faces go in the water when the swimmer decides they do.

Everything else follows from that: keep the group small enough that a scared kid gets real attention, and let the wall be an option for as long as they need it.

Every class starts with a free water assessment, so your kid lands in a group at the right level from day one.
