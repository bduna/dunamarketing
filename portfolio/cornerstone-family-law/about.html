About the firm

Someone steady, when it matters most.

I practice family law because the moments people come to me are the hardest of their lives — and they deserve someone steady.

Divorce, custody, and the questions that come with them can feel overwhelming and frightening. My work is to make them understandable: to lay out your options in plain language, answer the questions keeping you up at night, and help you move forward one clear step at a time.
