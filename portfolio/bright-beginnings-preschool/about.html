About us · Fort Wayne, Indiana

A preschool built around being read to

Bright Beginnings has been on the same footing since 2014: books twice a day, outside in every weather, and teachers who stay long enough to know your child.

Why we opened

I taught kindergarten for sixteen years and kept meeting four-year-olds who’d never been read to. Bright Beginnings opened in 2014 to fix that earlier.

That’s still the whole idea. Reading aloud twice a day isn’t an activity we schedule when there’s time left over — it’s the thing the rest of the day is built around. The same goes for getting outside: coats on, and out we go, in every weather.

And the third piece is the one parents notice last and value most. Our teachers stay for years, so the person who greets your two-year-old in September is the same person who knows exactly which book he wants in March.

Come and look at the room

We’d rather you saw it than read about it. The tour is free, the trial morning is free, and neither one is a sales pitch — it’s a normal morning with your child in it.

Bring your questions. We’re open Mon–Fri, 7am–6pm, and someone’s here from seven.
