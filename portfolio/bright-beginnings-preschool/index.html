It started in a kindergarten classroom

I taught kindergarten for sixteen years and kept meeting four-year-olds who’d never been read to. Bright Beginnings opened in 2014 to fix that earlier.

That’s still the whole idea: books twice a day, real outdoor time in every weather, and teachers who stay long enough to know your child.
