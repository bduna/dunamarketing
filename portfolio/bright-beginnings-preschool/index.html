For the parent going back to work

Leaving a two-year-old is hard. Here is what makes it easier.

Most of the parents who call us are going back to work and are more nervous about the first morning than their child will be. Four things tend to settle it.

  1. You’ll meet the person who will still be here in March.

    Our teachers stay for years. The person who takes your child’s coat in September is the same person who knows which book he wants by spring. One family had the same lead teacher for both their children, four years apart.

  2. You can try a whole morning first.

    The tour is free and so is the trial morning. Neither is a sales pitch — it is a normal morning with your child in it, and you get to watch how it goes before you sign anything.

  3. Someone’s here from seven.

    We’re open Monday to Friday, 7am to 6pm, and there is before-and-after care for the parents whose work doesn’t fit neatly inside those hours.

  4. You’ll know exactly what it costs.

    Every program has its price printed next to it — full-time toddler care is $290 a week, preschool for 3-to-5s is $250, half days are $160. No packages to decode, and nothing to ask awkwardly on the phone.
