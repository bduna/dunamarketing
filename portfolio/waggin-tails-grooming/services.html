Full groom · from $65

The whole works.

Bath, a haircut to suit the breed or the look you want, nails, and a finishing brush-out. It’s the service most dogs come in for and the one that takes the longest, which is why the price moves with size and coat.

Long coats, doodles, double coats: bring the tangles. Just tell me about them on the phone so I set aside the right amount of time.

Bath & brush · from $35

Clean and brushed, no haircut.

A proper wash, a full dry, and a thorough brush-out. The right call between full grooms, before a trip, or for a coat that doesn’t need cutting — just attention.

De-shed treatment · from $45

For the dogs that shed on everything.

A bath and blow-out built to lift the loose undercoat out of the coat here, before it lands on your couch, your car and your black trousers. Huskies, shepherds, labs, and anything else with an undercoat to blow.

If you can see the seasonal shed coming, that’s the week to book it.

Puppy’s first groom · $40

A good first memory of the table.

Short, gentle, and more about trust than the haircut. Your puppy gets used to the water, the dryer and my hands before any of it needs to be perfect — so the second visit, and every one after it, is easy.

Nail trim · $15

Quick, calm, and done right.

On its own or added to any visit. If your dog has strong feelings about their feet, say so — I’ll go slowly, one paw at a time, and stop when they’ve had enough for one day.

“He comes home fluffy, happy, and smelling amazing.”
