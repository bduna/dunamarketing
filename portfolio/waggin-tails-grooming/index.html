About Waggin' Tails

Fifteen years, and I still love a good tail wag.

I've groomed dogs for fifteen years, and I still get a little excited every time a nervous pup leaves wagging. I opened Waggin' Tails in 2017 to do it my way — patient, gentle, and cage-free.

That means your dog waits with me, not in a crate, and we go at a pace they're comfortable with. It's why anxious and rescue dogs tend to settle in here.
