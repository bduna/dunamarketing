The card in the front of the book

Somebody here read it first.

Every book on the picks shelf carries a handwritten recommendation card — one per book, signed by whoever read it. Those cards are the thing people actually trust, and the reason most of our regulars leave with something they weren’t looking for.

A third of the space, better taste

“I worked at a chain bookstore for eight years and watched it close.”

In 2017 I opened Dog-Eared in the same neighborhood, with a third of the space and better taste. New releases sit at cover price. Used books start at $4. If we don’t have the thing you want, we’ll special-order it in three to five days.

Book club is the first Thursday of the month, and gift wrapping is free, whatever you’re buying.
