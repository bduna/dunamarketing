Services & prices

Five things we do, and what each one costs.

Prices are what you pay for the work as listed. Nail art is priced per nail, so one accent finger costs a lot less than all ten — tell us which when you call.

Price list

Classic manicure Shape, cuticles and one coat of the colour you picked at the desk. The everyday one.
