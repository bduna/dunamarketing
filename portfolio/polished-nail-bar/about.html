About

How Polished started

Three years in an apartment, then six chairs.

I did nails out of my apartment for three years while working retail. I opened Polished in 2021 with two chairs; now we have six, and a waitlist.

What we’re known for is nail art that actually looks like the picture you brought in — hot pink, cream, and a little chrome.
