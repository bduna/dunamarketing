About

Twelve years chasing soft light.

I’ve been shooting weddings and portraits for twelve years. I chase soft, natural light — the kind you get from a north window.

I keep sessions relaxed. No stiff poses, no long shot lists to march through — just enough direction to let people be themselves, and the light doing most of the work. What you get back looks like the actual person, not a performance of them.
