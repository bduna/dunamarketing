How a ring gets made here.

You sit down at the bench. We talk about what you have in mind, and I sketch — usually more than one idea, so there is something to choose between. Then the ring is made here, on this bench, and you pick it up in the shop.

Repairs run the same way: you hand the piece across the counter, we look at it together, and you get a straight answer about what it needs before any work starts.

Why custom

Nothing here comes from a mall case.

A case ring is a guess at what a thousand people might like. This shop works the other way around: one person, one hand, one ring that exists because you asked for it. That has been the trade here since 1961, and it is why the work stays on Grand Avenue.

The bench is open Tuesday through Saturday.
