I got tired of ten-minute exams.

I opened Clearview in 2016 because I was tired of ten-minute exams. Mine take forty minutes and I actually explain what I’m looking at.

Long, unhurried exams, and frames that don’t all look the same. That’s the whole idea.

What forty minutes buys.

A ten-minute exam checks whether you can read the chart. Forty minutes is long enough to dilate properly, look at the health of the retina, talk through what has changed since last time, and explain what the numbers on the prescription actually mean.

It also means there is time to try frames without being rushed at the end. Nobody is waiting in the corridor for the room, because the room is booked for you.
