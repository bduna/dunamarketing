Since 2017

Started by a hospice nurse

Wellspring exists because families kept asking one nurse a question she couldn't answer. This is how she answers it now.

I was a hospice nurse for thirteen years. I started Wellspring in 2017 because families kept asking me who they could trust, and I didn't have a good answer.

Founder, Wellspring Home Health

Thirteen years of hospice nursing teaches you what good care looks like from the inside — and how quickly it falls apart when the person at the door keeps changing.

So Wellspring is built around one thing: matching the same caregiver to the same family, week after week. Every caregiver is background-checked and insured before they ever set foot in a client's home.

How we work

Four things we can say plainly, because they're either true or they aren't.

  1. The same caregiver

    Matched to your family and kept there, week after week — not whoever is free that morning.

  2. Checked and insured

    Every caregiver is background-checked and insured. Every one, not most.

  3. A free assessment first

    We come to the house, look at what's actually needed, and tell you plainly what we'd do.

  4. Available around the clock

    The office runs Monday to Friday, 8am–6pm. Caregivers are available 24/7.

What families tell us

Every caregiver is background-checked and insured, and care starts at a four-hour minimum visit — hourly, overnight, or live-in.
