How we work

What happens after you call.

You get a person, not an answering service. Describe what the drain or the heater is doing and we can usually tell you on the phone whether it is a tonight problem or a Tuesday problem — and we will say so honestly, because a call we talk you out of is cheaper than a customer we lose.

Drain cleaning starts at $89, and that is a flat starting price rather than an hourly meter running while we work. Water heater installs start at $1,400 including the unit. Leak repair is quoted once we can see it, because a leak behind a wall and a leak under a sink are not the same job.

Two trucks means we are never so booked that a real emergency waits until morning. It also means the person who quotes the work is usually the person who does it.
