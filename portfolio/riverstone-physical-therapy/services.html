What we charge.

  • Initial evaluation

    $150

    Your first hour. We work out what’s actually failing and why, and you leave knowing the plan rather than a photocopied sheet of exercises.

  • Follow-up visits

    $95

    Every visit after the evaluation — the same therapist, the same full hour, one-on-one.

  • Dry needling

    $40

    An add-on to a visit when it’s the right tool for what we found.

  • Post-surgical rehab

    By quote

    Six weeks out, twelve weeks out, and every case different — so it’s quoted rather than priced off a list. Call and tell us how far out of surgery you are and what you’re trying to get back to.
