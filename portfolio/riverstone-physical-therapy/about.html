The clinic I wish I’d had.

I blew out my own knee playing college soccer and hated every minute of my rehab. Riverstone is the clinic I wish I’d had.

What that turned into, in practice: one-on-one hour-long sessions — you never get handed off to an aide.

It means the person who tests your knee is the person who watches it week after week, and can tell you what changed. Most of the people who walk in are somewhere in the middle of a recovery they didn’t choose, and are quietly wondering whether they get their sport back. That question deserves an actual answer, not a handout.
