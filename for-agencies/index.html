Built for high-volume agencies

Website sales scale with your sales team. Production doesn’t.

Every website an agency sells still has to be written, designed, built, checked and handed off. At volume, that work sets the ceiling: content chased from clients, builds waiting in a queue, and hours that come out of the margin on every site. Duna Marketing takes production off your team, so the number of websites you can sell stops depending on the number of people you can hire.

Built for agencies that:

  • sell websites as a product line, on their own or inside marketing packages
  • deliver sites for local and small businesses at volume
  • keep the client relationship and want production to stay invisible
  • would rather add capacity than headcount

The operating model

One production path, the same for every site.

Your team keeps the client. We take each site from questionnaire to transfer.

  1. Intake

    Your team tells us the type of business, and we send the questionnaire for that business type.

  2. Questionnaire

    It’s a Word document your team passes along. Your client completes it in about 20 minutes and sends it back with their photos. We never contact your client.

  3. Production

    We write every page fresh from the answers, design and build the site mobile-first, and run it through our quality checks. Within 24 hours of the completed questionnaire coming back, the complete site is ready for your team’s review.

  4. Review and transfer

    Your team sends changes, with five revision rounds included. Your client approves, your team signs off, and we transfer the site to your agency.

Quality at volume

Every page is checked by software, then reviewed by a person.

Duna Marketing runs on its own AI-powered production system, designed and built by founder Barrett Duna. It turns a completed questionnaire into a complete multipage site, then holds every page to the same checks before your team sees it.

How the checks work

Facts
The business name, phone number, email, services and prices are checked against the client’s answers, with prices shown exactly as given.
Page essentials
No placeholder text, every image present with alt text, and a title, a meta description and exactly one H1 on every page.
Craft
Defects a text check misses, such as content printed twice, preview cards with no real copy, or a font weight the font doesn’t include.
Contrast
Measured from the pixels behind each line of text on the rendered page, so text over a photo has to clear a 4.5:1 ratio.
Screenshots
Every page rendered at desktop and phone widths for review.
A person
Reviews every page before it reaches your team.

FAQ

Answers for agency leadership.

Anything else: text or call (707) 385-5673, or email barrett@dunamarketing.com.

How many websites can Duna Marketing produce?

Up to 600 websites a month. Each site is ready for your team’s review within 24 hours of its completed questionnaire coming back, at any volume up to that capacity.

How does quality hold across hundreds of sites?

Every site runs through the same process: a questionnaire for its business type, every page written fresh from the answers, a mobile-first build, automated checks for facts, craft and contrast, and a person reviewing every page before your team sees it.

Will our clients know you built their sites?

No. Sites are built under your agency’s name, ours appears nowhere on them, and we never contact your clients. Everything runs through your team.

What does our team provide for each site?

The type of business, so we can send the right questionnaire. Then the completed questionnaire and the client’s photos.

How do revisions work?

Five revision rounds are included with every site. Your team sends the changes, your client approves, your team signs off, and we transfer the site.

Who owns the finished websites?

Your agency. Each site is transferred to you in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or in WordPress. We don’t host it: you host it wherever you like, and any developer can edit it.

How is pricing structured?

Volume-based partnership pricing, quoted for each agency. It starts with a partnership conversation.

How does a partnership start?

With a paid pilot batch: a smaller first set of your client sites, built through the full process. After the pilot, production ramps to your full volume.
