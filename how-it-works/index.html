How it works

From a text to a live site in four steps.

No sales call, no proposal, nothing to prepare. Here’s exactly what happens after you text me.

  1. Text or call me

    One tap from any page. I’ll answer your questions, tell you the price, and send you the questionnaire. Half the price is due to start the build.

  2. Answer the questionnaire

    About 20 minutes on your phone. It asks about your business in plain English: what you do, who you serve, what things cost, the story behind it. Tap the microphone and talk if typing is a pain.

  3. I build it, you get a preview link

    Within 24 hours of your answers coming in, you get a link to your site. Every word on it comes from what you told me. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. You approve, it goes live

    When you say yes, the second half is due and I put the site on your domain. Hosting is included, with no monthly fee.

What I’ll need from you

Four things. Two are optional.

  • Your questionnaire answersAbout 20 minutes. This is what your site gets written from.
  • Photos, if you have themOptional. If you don’t, I’ll source them.
  • Your logo, if you have oneOptional. I’ll work without one.
  • Access to your domain at launchIf you already have a domain, I’ll need to get into where it’s registered to point it at the site. If you don’t, buying one takes a few minutes and I’ll walk you through it.
What you won’t need

Nothing else.

  • A designer or a copywriterThe design and every word on the site are part of the build.
  • A hosting planHosting is included. There’s no monthly fee.
  • Any tech skillsYou answer questions. I put it online.
  • A meetingEverything happens by text, with a call if you’d rather talk.
