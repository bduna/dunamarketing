The process

From a text to a live site in four steps.

No sales call, no proposal, nothing to prepare. Here is exactly what happens after you text or call me, and what I need from you at each point.

  1. Text or call me

    One tap from any page on this site reaches Barrett, not a form. I answer your questions and send you the questionnaire, and we agree on the quote before anything starts.

  2. Answer the questionnaire

    A document you fill in, about 20 minutes of your time. It asks about your business in plain English: what you do, who you serve, what things cost, the story behind it. Short, honest answers beat polished ones.

  3. I build it, you get a preview link

    Within 24 hours of your answers coming in, a link lands in your phone. Every word on it comes from what you told me. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. You approve, it goes live

    When you say yes, I put the site live on your domain and look after the hosting from there.

What I need from you

Four things. Two are optional.

  • Your questionnaire answersAbout 20 minutes. This is what your site gets written from.
  • Photos, if you have themOptional. If you don’t, I’ll source them.
  • Your logo, if you have oneOptional. I’ll work without one.
  • Access to your domain at launchSo I can point it at the finished site. No domain yet? Buying one takes a few minutes and I’ll walk you through it.

What you won’t need

Nothing else.

  • A designer or a copywriterThe design and every word on the site are part of the build.
  • A hosting planHosting is handled as part of the build.
  • Any tech skillsYou answer questions. I put it online.
  • A meetingEverything happens by text, with a call if you would rather talk.

While you are working

You never have to sit down for this.

The talking happens by text or call, between jobs, on the phone in your pocket. The questionnaire is a document you fill in whenever you get a quiet twenty minutes. The preview arrives as a link, and approving it is a reply.
