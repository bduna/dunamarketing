How it works

How a website job runs, from your first text to the handoff.

You keep the client and the relationship. I build the site, and everything runs through you. Here’s exactly who does what, and when.

  1. You send me the job.

    Text or call and tell me what kind of business it is. That’s all I need to start, and there’s no brief for you to write. I create a questionnaire for that type of business.

    You
    Tell me what kind of business it is.
    Me
    Create the questionnaire for that type of business.

  2. Your client answers the questionnaire.

    I send you the questionnaire document to pass along to your client. It takes about 20 minutes and asks what a good copywriter would ask. Your client sends it back along with their photos.

    You
    Pass the questionnaire along to your client.
    Your client
    Answers it in about 20 minutes and sends it back with their photos.

  3. I build the site. You review it within 24 hours.

    Every page is written fresh from the answers, not pasted from them. Their photos go in place, the site is built mobile-first, and I check it page by page before you see it. What arrives is the complete multipage site, not a wireframe or a homepage draft.

    Me
    Write every page, build the site and check it page by page.
    You
    Review the finished site, within 24 hours of the questionnaire coming back.

  4. Your client approves, you sign off, and I transfer the site.

    Send me the changes and I make them. Once your client approves and you sign off, I transfer the website to you and I’m out of the picture. From then on, it’s yours.

    You
    Send me any changes, then sign off.
    Your client
    Approves the site.
    Me
    Make the changes, then transfer the website to you.

What you get back

A finished site in plain files, with nothing tied to me.

  • The complete multipage site, with every page written
  • Your client’s photos in place
  • Built mobile-first and checked page by page
  • Plain HTML, CSS and a few kilobytes of script
  • No subscription, and nothing to renew
  • Any developer can edit it, and you host it wherever you like

White-label, start to finish
