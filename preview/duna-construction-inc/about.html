About

I started when I was sixteen.

I worked with my dad for many years, starting at sixteen years old. At twenty-one I decided to get my contractor's license, and since then I have been out on my own at Duna Construction Inc.

What I do now is build houses and remodel them, in Cloverdale and around Sonoma County. Both come to me the same way and both get quoted the same way: I go and look at the job first.

The work itself arrives by word of mouth. Somebody had a house built or a house remodelled, and they tell the next person about it. There is no shortcut to that kind of recommendation — you get it by doing an excellent job, and then you get it again on the next one.

Who I do my best work for

Quality craftsmanship, and a fair wage for it.

That is the arrangement I look for, and I would rather say it plainly at the start than find out halfway through a build that we wanted different things.

It is also the reason the pricing works the way it does. A quote written against your actual house is an honest number. A price pulled off a list, before anyone has walked the property, is a guess that somebody has to pay for later.

Who you would be dealing with

Me. Barrett Duna. The person who comes out to look at your job is the person whose name is on the company, and he is the one you keep dealing with after the quote is signed.
