Process

From questionnaire to transferred website, the same way every time.

Every site follows one production path, at any volume up to 600 sites a month. Your team keeps the client. Here is who does what, and when.

The production path

Five stages, with every role spelled out.

  1. Intake

    Your team tells us the type of business. That’s all we need to start: there’s no brief to write.

    Your team
    Names the type of business.
    Duna Marketing
    Sends the questionnaire for that business type.

  2. Questionnaire

    A Word document tailored to the business type, asking what a good copywriter would ask. Your client completes it in about 20 minutes and returns it with their photos.

    Your team
    Passes the questionnaire to the client.
    Your client
    Completes it and returns it with photos.

  3. Production

    Every page is written fresh from the answers, never pasted from them. The site is designed for that business, built mobile-first and put through the automated checks, then a person reviews every page. What reaches your team is the complete multipage site, not a wireframe or a homepage draft.

    Duna Marketing
    Writes, designs, builds and checks every page.
    Your team
    Reviews the complete site within 24 hours of the completed questionnaire coming back.

    Pages from three of the samples, on a phone

  4. Revisions

    Your team sends the changes, with five revision rounds included. Your client approves, then your team signs off.

    Your team
    Sends changes and signs off.
    Your client
    Approves the site.
    Duna Marketing
    Makes the changes.

  5. Transfer

    We transfer the finished site to your agency in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or in WordPress. From then on it’s yours: host it wherever you like, and any developer can edit it.

    Duna Marketing
    Transfers the site.
    Your agency
    Owns and hosts it.

Quality checks

What every page is held to before your team sees it.

Production runs on Duna Marketing’s own AI-powered build system. These checks run on every site, and a person reviews every page after them.

Facts
The business name, phone number, email, services and prices are checked against the client’s answers. Prices must appear exactly as given, the phone number and email must link, and no placeholder text may remain.
Page essentials
Every page carries a title, a meta description and exactly one H1. Every image exists and has alt text, every in-page link resolves, and no heading repeats on a page.
Craft
Defects a text check misses: content printed twice, preview cards with no real copy, a page that’s only a heading, a font weight the font doesn’t include.
Contrast
Measured on the rendered page from the pixels behind each line of text. Text over a photo or gradient must clear 4.5:1, and every button needs an edge that stands out from the page.
Screenshots
Every page rendered 1280 pixels wide for desktop and 390 for phones, for the page-by-page review.
A person
Reviews every page before delivery.
